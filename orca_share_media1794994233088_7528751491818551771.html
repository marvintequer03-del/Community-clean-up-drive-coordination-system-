About the System

Community Clean-Up Drive Coordination System

This web-based system helps administrators, volunteers, and barangay officials organize community clean-up activities.


Purpose: To make event creation, volunteer registration, and scheduling easier.


Process: Login → Dashboard → View Events → Volunteer Registration → Schedule → Clean-Up Drive.
